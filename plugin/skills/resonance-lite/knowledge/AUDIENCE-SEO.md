# Audience and SEO

> Rules by Mariano "Nano" Pfeiffer, developed for the Resonance channel. MIT License.

## What this audience wants
- **Long-form.** People press play and leave it for hours. 90 minutes minimum, ideally 1-3 hours.
- **Total immersion.** Success means the listener feels they are right there: by the fire, under the rain.
- **No surprises.** No bangs, no volume jumps, no hard cuts. Whatever startles = dislike and close.
- **Invisible loops.** If it noticeably starts over, the trance breaks.
- **Niche consistency.** Better 200 very specific videos in a few niches than 3000 generic ones.

## Immersion rubric (check the plan against it before the checkpoint)
1. No violent loop: sound and image loop without a perceptible seam.
2. No silences between elements.
3. Dynamics match the use (bounded wave for sleep, constant for focus and playlists).
4. No booms: compressed dynamics, controlled peaks.
5. "You are there": the scene is believable and enveloping.
6. Time variation: over 20 minutes, subtle micro-variation so it never feels like a 2-minute loop.
7. Title follows the formula below.
8. It belongs to a niche, not generic.

## Title formula
`[Main keyword] [one emoji, optional] {duration} | [benefit / use cases]`

- Use the literal placeholder `{duration}`: rlite replaces it with the real length ("90 Min", "2 Hours").
- Main keyword first. At most one emoji. Use cases at the end (Sleep, Study, Relax, Focus).
- **Never write "4K"**: the master is 1080p and that would be misleading (rlite blocks it).
- Examples:
  - `Fireplace 🔥 {duration} Crackling Fire Sounds | Sleep, Relax & Study`
  - `Rain on a Tent ⛺ {duration} | Sleep, ASMR, Camping Ambience`
  - `Deep Sleep Piano · {duration} of Calm Felt Piano | Sleep, Relax`

## Description
2-4 short lines: what it is, what it is for, "headphones recommended / cast it to your TV".
rlite appends chapters (playlists) and the channel footer.

## Base tags by niche
- **Sleep**: sleep music, deep sleep, insomnia relief, black screen, healing
- **ASMR / sound**: asmr, [element] sounds, relaxing sounds, white noise, ambience
- **Focus**: focus music, study music, concentration, deep work, binaural
- **Fireplace**: fireplace, crackling fire, cozy fire, fireplace on tv

Keep tags under 500 characters in total.

## Niches that work (highest demand first)
fireplace · rain (tent / window / forest) · ocean waves at night · thunderstorm · deep sleep piano ·
forest and birds · coffee shop · white / brown noise · binaural focus · winter cabin · night crickets ·
lofi study playlists.

Recommend the user picks 2-3 niches and stays consistent.

## YouTube policy (tell the user, do not hide it)
- Mass-produced, low-variation AI content can be demonetized or removed ("inauthentic content",
  clarified July 2026). Each video must be curated by the user: unique scene, unique metadata.
- Photorealistic AI visuals must be disclosed as "altered or synthetic content" in Studio.
- Monetization needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours: the first months are cost only.
