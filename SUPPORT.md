# Support

**Private beta.** Support is direct: message the maintainer, or open an issue in this repository.

- **Bug:** open an issue with the *Bug report* template and paste the output of `rlite doctor --report` (it never includes keys or personal paths).
- **After each video:** open an issue with the *Beta feedback* template.
- **Blocking problem:** message the maintainer directly.

Not supported: account or billing problems with providers (ElevenLabs, Apiframe, Higgsfield), YouTube monetization or strikes, and legal advice.
