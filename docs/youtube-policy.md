# YouTube policies you should know

This is not legal advice. Read YouTube's own pages before monetizing.

- **Inauthentic content.** In July 2026 YouTube clarified that mass-produced content "made easily
  with AI, CGI or templates, with little variation from video to video" is not eligible for
  monetization and can be removed. Resonance Lite keeps a human checkpoint on every video, varies
  scenes and thumbnails, and has no bulk mode, but nothing guarantees eligibility. Curate.
- **Altered or synthetic content.** Realistic AI-generated visuals must be disclosed in YouTube
  Studio. `UPLOAD.md` tells you when to tick the box (by default for animated and still visuals).
- **Partner Program.** Monetization requires 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 public watch hours in a year.
- **Music rights.** ElevenLabs paid plans include commercial use. Suno via Apiframe gives usage
  rights, not ownership, through an account that is not yours: high risk for monetized channels.
  `final/provenance.json` records where each piece came from, in case of a claim.
